Why it matters now

Private label and distributor channels multiply every product change

A single fragrance refill can appear under Vectair's brand, under a distributor's label, on jan-san marketplaces and in a facilities-management catalog. Each one has its own naming, image and attribute rules. When a refill is reformulated or a dispenser is updated, the change has to reach all of them, or the wrong version keeps selling.

Jan-san distributors

Boelter, Imperial Dade-type distributors and buying groups each want a product feed in their own template, with their own attribute lists.

Private label

The same product under a partner brand needs a second, controlled version of every title, image and description.

Regulatory content

SDS sheets, fragrance allergen data and regional compliance documents have to stay attached to the right SKU in every market.

The pattern we see

Where product content usually breaks down

We have not audited Vectair's systems. What follows is the pattern we see at manufacturers selling through jan-san distribution and the questions we would like to check against yours.

No single record

  • Product content lives in spreadsheets, a website CMS, the ERP and marketing's design files
  • Sales, marketing and the regional offices each hold a different version
  • Distributors ask for the latest spec sheet and someone has to go find it

Manual distributor feeds

  • Each distributor and marketplace wants a different template, so the same SKU is re-keyed several times
  • Private-label versions are maintained by hand alongside the Vectair-branded ones
  • Errors surface at the distributor, after the fact

Problem by problem

How Salsify fits Vectair

Each row maps a likely issue to the specific part of the platform that handles it.

The issue

No single record

Spec sheets, images and copy are scattered across regions, systems and inboxes.

What Salsify does

PIM as the system of record

One product record per SKU with regional and private-label variants managed as controlled versions. US, EMEA and marketing teams edit the same record with role-based permissions.

What changes

One answer for every distributor question, owned by product, visible to sales.

The issue

Every distributor wants its own feed

Templates, portals and emailed spreadsheets for each jan-san partner and marketplace.

What Salsify does

Syndication with requirements built in

Salsify maps Vectair's core attributes to each distributor's and marketplace's format and publishes from the same record.

What changes

Enter once, publish everywhere. A new distributor is a new mapping, not a new project.

The issue

Private label drift

Partner-branded versions fall out of sync with the Vectair original after a change.

What Salsify does

Variant management

Private-label SKUs inherit from the master record; only the branded fields differ. A change to the master flows to every variant through workflow.

What changes

No more partner catalogs selling a spec you retired last year.

The issue

Regulatory documents go stale

SDS, allergen and compliance documents are attached to products inconsistently by region.

What Salsify does

DAM tied to the record

Documents are stored against the SKU with versioning and expiry, and published with the product to every channel that needs them.

What changes

The current SDS is the only one anyone can find.

The issue

Acquired lines arrive in their own format

WizKid and future acquisitions bring separate catalogs and conventions.

What Salsify does

Flexible data model and workflows

New lines are onboarded into the same model with mapping and AI-assisted attribute extraction from existing spec sheets.

What changes

One governed catalog, however many brands it holds.

What a product change looks like with Salsify

1. Change onceA product manager updates a spec, image or description in Salsify, or it flows in from the ERP and marketing's design files.
2. WorkflowThe right approvers sign off in their queue. Rules apply automatically.
3. ValidateReadiness reports check the record against every destination's current spec and image rules.
4. PublishOne click sends the correct format to every distributor, marketplace and private-label partner.
5. MeasurePage grades and compliance scores come back into Salsify so the team sees what landed.
